  1. 01

    Start a game night

    Run /gamenight in your Discord server, or create one on the web. Either way, your group gets one place to answer.

    • Name it, pick the game and how long you'll play
    • Choose the dates and times that might work
    • Invite your players with one link
  2. 02

    Find the overlap

    Players link Google Calendar or mark their availability by hand. Nobody has to compare screenshots of their week.

    • Linking Google Calendar is optional
    • We only see free and busy blocks, never your events
    • Rather not link? Tap the times that work for you
  3. 03

    See the best times

    GameNightly ranks every time by how many people are free, so the best night is right at the top.

    • The heat map runs from purple (just you) to gold (everyone)
    • Times with the most players rise to the top
    • See who's free or busy at each time
  4. 04

    Lock it in

    The organizer picks a time, and GameNightly adds the event to their Google Calendar and invites the players.

    • One event on the organizer's calendar
    • Players get a calendar invite
    • Then all that's left is to play
