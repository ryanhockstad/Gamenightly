Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

GameNightly ("we", "us") helps groups schedule game nights through our Discord bot and website. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

From Discord, when you use the bot:

  • Your Discord user ID, and the ID of the server and channel where a game night is planned.
  • Game night details: the game, who was invited, the suggested times, which players are free or busy at each time, and the time that was confirmed.
  • Your time zone, if you set one. Server admins can also set a default time zone and other defaults for their server.

From Google, only if you choose to link your Google account:

  • Your Google account email address.
  • The time zone of your primary Google Calendar.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, so we can check your availability when you join a game night.
  • Free/busy information for your primary calendar. We read it when we're looking for times, use it right away to rank them, and don't store it. We never see the titles, descriptions, locations or attendees of your events.

From the website, when you use a scheduling page:

  • The display name you type in and the times you mark as available.
  • The session details the organizer enters, like the title, dates and time window.

We don't use advertising trackers, and we don't sell information that identifies you. We may create and share aggregated, de-identified insights. See Aggregated insights below.

How we use it

  • To find times when the players in a game night are free and rank them.
  • When an organizer confirms a time, to create that event on the organizer's primary Google Calendar and add the players who linked Google as guests. Google then sends them the invitation.
  • To show times in each person's time zone.
  • To run, secure and fix the service.
  • To create aggregated, de-identified insights about how people play, as described below.

Google user data

GameNightly's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We use Google Calendar data only to provide the scheduling features described above.
  • We don't sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • We don't transfer it to anyone else, except as needed to provide the service, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • No one reads your Google data, except with your permission, for security reasons, or when the law requires it.

Google user data is never used in our aggregated insights, even in aggregated or de-identified form.

The permissions we request:

  • calendar.events.freebusy: lets us see when you're busy, so we can suggest free times.
  • calendar.events: lets us create the confirmed game night event on the organizer's calendar and read your calendar's time zone.
  • userinfo.email: lets us see your email address, to identify your linked account and invite you to events.

Aggregated insights

GameNightly is free and has no ads. To keep it that way, we may combine information about game nights scheduled on our website and turn it into aggregated, de-identified insights. Examples: which games are popular, which days and times groups usually play, typical group sizes, and broad regional trends (country or state level). We may share or sell these insights to businesses such as game publishers, retailers and researchers.

How we protect you:

  • Website data only. Insights come only from information you give us directly on the GameNightly website. We never use Google user data or data we receive from Discord's API, in any form.
  • No individual data. Insights never include your name, email, account IDs, or anything that identifies you or your group. We only report figures that combine many groups, never a single game night.
  • No re-identification. We won't try to re-identify de-identified data, and we contractually forbid anyone who receives it from trying.
  • Your choice. You can ask us to leave your data out of insights at any time (see Your choices).

If we ever want to share information that could identify you, we'll ask for your permission first. It will be off unless you turn it on.

How we store and protect it

  • Google tokens are encrypted before they're stored, and only the one service that talks to Google can read them.
  • Data is stored on servers we run and with our hosting providers, and is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we keep and who can reach it.

How long we keep it

  • Discord game nights are deleted automatically 14 days after their last change.
  • Website scheduling sessions are deleted automatically 30 days after their last date.
  • Your linked Google account (email, time zone and tokens) is kept until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

  • Revoke Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that we can't read your calendar or create events on it.
  • Delete your data: email us from the address you linked, or tell us your Discord username, and we'll delete your stored data within 30 days.
  • You can use GameNightly without linking Google by marking your availability by hand.
  • Leave your data out of aggregated insights at any time by emailing us. This applies going forward. Insights already created can't include you individually, because they are de-identified.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia or Texas), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct or delete it; and
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, or its use for targeted advertising. We don't sell or share information that identifies you, and we don't do targeted advertising.

We offer these rights to everyone, wherever you live. To use them, email us. We'll confirm the request comes from you, respond within 45 days, and won't treat you differently for using them. If we turn down a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer.

Who we share it with

  • Other players in your game night see your name, whether you're free at the suggested times, and the confirmed time. They don't see your calendar.
  • Google and Discord, which handle the parts of the service that run on their platforms under their own privacy policies.
  • Hosting providers that run our infrastructure for us.
  • Business partners, who may receive aggregated, de-identified insights, never information that identifies you.
  • A buyer or successor, if GameNightly is part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. We'll tell you first, and this policy will still apply to your data.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Children

GameNightly isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll also announce it in our Discord bot or on this website.
